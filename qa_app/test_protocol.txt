Clinical Study Protocol - Brief Synopsis

Protocol ID: MSC-2026-007       Sponsor: Mascot Therapeutics, Inc.

1. Background and Rationale
Heart failure with reduced ejection fraction (HFrEF) affects approximately
6.5 million paitents in the United States. Despite optimal medical therapy,
mortality remains hgih and quality of life is significantly impaired. This
study will evaluate the safety and efficacy of MSC-007, a novel oral agent,
in adult patients with symptomatic HFrEF.

2. Objectives
Primary: To evaluate the change from baseline in NT-proBNP at Week 12.
Secondary: To assess functional capacity (6-minute walk distance) and
patient-reported outcomes.

3. Study Design
This is a randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled, parallel-group
Phase 2 trial. Approximately 240 subjects will be enrolled. Subjects will
be randomized 1:1 to MSC-007 50 mg once daily or matching placebo. All
participants will receive standard background therapy. Patients will be
followed for 14 weeks.

4. Eligibility Criteria
Inclusion: Adults aged 18-80 with NYHA class II-III HFrEF; LVEF <= 40%;
on stable guideline-directed medical therapy for at least 3 months.
Exclusion: eGFR < 30 mL/min/1.73 m^2; acute coronary syndrome within
30 days; pregnancy; participation in another interventional trial within
60 days.

5. Statistical Considerations
The primary analysis will compare the mean change in NT-proBNP between
treatment groups using an ANCOVA model adjusted for baseline. We expect
MSC-007 to reduce NT-proBNP by 30%. Statistical significance will be
declared if p < 0.05.

6. Safety Reporting
Adverse events will be collected throughout the study. Serious adverse
events should be reported to the sponsor.

7. Ethics
This study will be conducted in accordance with the Declaration of
Helsinki. All patients will provide written informed consent prior to
study procedures.

Primary endpoint: To evaluate the change from baseline in NT-proBNP at Week 12.
